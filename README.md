# Path integrals in the pricing of path-dependent options

Bachelor's thesis in Physics (TFG), University of Oviedo, 2026–27.
Roberto Cepeda Rocandio, fifth-year student in the double degree in Mathematics and Physics.
Supervisor: Diego Rodríguez Gómez (Theoretical Physics, University of Oviedo).

The thesis asks whether path integrals can give a single framework for pricing path-dependent options (Asian, barrier and lookback options) in the Black–Scholes model, where the martingale approach needs a different technique for each payoff. In the variables x = log S and τ = T − t, the Black–Scholes equation becomes a Schrödinger equation in imaginary time, so prices can be written as Euclidean path integrals (Feynman–Kac) and the path dependence enters as a potential or a boundary condition.

It can be seen as a continuation of my bachelor's thesis in Mathematics, which develops the foundations of option pricing with martingales: https://github.com/indexxxxbraker/option-pricing-via-martingales
